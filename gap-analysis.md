# Gap Analysis

All conditions below are hypothetical assumptions, not verified findings about a real service.

1. **Privacy governance:** incomplete data inventory, notice and retention evidence. Potential impact: unclear processing, excessive retention and legal/reputational exposure.
2. **Access governance:** privileged MFA and periodic access reviews not consistently evidenced. Potential impact: unauthorised access or changes to citizen records.
3. **Vulnerability management:** risk-based patching and security testing not consistently governed. Potential impact: exploitation, data exposure or service disruption.
4. **Logging and monitoring:** retention, time synchronisation and alert ownership need validation. Potential impact: delayed detection and weak investigation evidence.
5. **Incident response:** playbook and exercises require improvement. Potential impact: slow containment and delayed reporting where applicable.
6. **Recovery:** backup restoration and RPO/RTO evidence need strengthening. Potential impact: prolonged interruption or data loss.
7. **Supplier oversight:** due diligence and contract security clauses require improvement. Potential impact: third-party compromise.
8. **Policies and awareness:** policies and training are inconsistent. Potential impact: avoidable staff errors and unclear accountability.
