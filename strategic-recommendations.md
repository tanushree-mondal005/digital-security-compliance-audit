# Strategic Recommendations

1. Assign service, security and privacy owners; maintain a risk register.
2. Apply least privilege, role-based access, MFA for privileged accounts and quarterly access reviews.
3. Map personal data, minimise collection, publish clear notices, set retention/deletion rules and encrypt sensitive data.
4. Adopt secure development, code/dependency scanning, risk-based patching and authorised security testing.
5. Centralise and protect logs, synchronise system time and assign alert-monitoring responsibilities.
6. Test incident response, escalation, evidence preservation and applicable CERT-In reporting processes.
7. Define RPO/RTO, isolate backups and test restoration regularly.
8. Assess suppliers and include security, breach notification, access and exit clauses in contracts.
9. Approve version-controlled policies and provide role-based staff training.
10. Track measurable security KPIs and close high-risk findings first.
