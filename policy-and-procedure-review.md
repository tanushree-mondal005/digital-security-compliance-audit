# Policy and Procedure Review

Recommended controlled documents:
- Information Security Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Data Protection and Retention Policy
- Secure Development and Change Management Procedure
- Vulnerability and Patch Management Procedure
- Logging and Monitoring Standard
- Incident Response Plan
- Backup and Business Continuity Plan
- Third-Party Risk Management Procedure

Each document should include an owner, approval authority, version, effective date, review date, responsibilities and evidence of staff communication. Policies should be tested through exercises and control checks.
